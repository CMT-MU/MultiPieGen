Harmonics of point groups#

Harmonics of all 47 point groups (32 crystallographic point groups, 5 other settings “-1”, and 10 complex groups “-c”). Each PDF lists the harmonics up to rank 11, with the polar (Q) and axial (G) symbols side by side. The expression is that of the polar harmonics.

The PDFs are created by python -m multipiegen.create_pdf.create_pdf_harmonics in multipiegen/pdf/PG/{no}-{tag}/.
