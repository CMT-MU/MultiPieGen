Changes from MultiPie Ver.2 (multipole data)#

This note lists what MultiPieGen does differently from MultiPie Ver.2 for the multipole-keyed data: conventions, algorithms, and the resulting differences. The algorithms of MultiPieGen themselves are described in Generation of the multipole data. The numbers below come from a comparison with MultiPie Ver.2 made on 2026-11-11 (develop 369c8bf). A Japanese version is here.

not held; looked up by (X, l, Gamma, n) of the key in harmonics.pkl (values: multipolar harmonics (internal_coefficient, multipole_expression), atomic multipole group matrix, cluster SAMB samb)

  • The E2g sign change is a change of the top harmonics. It carries into the D6h-family subgroups (rank-4 E multiplets of 16 hexagonal/trigonal groups) and into everything built on the harmonics.

  • The gv sign change is a change of the orbital (and of the response-tensor multipole); the top harmonics of Oh are unchanged, and the harmonics of the Oh family agree with MultiPie Ver.2.

  • In the data compared so far, the harmonics multiplicity n happened to coincide with MultiPie Ver.2’s renumbered n (multipolar harmonics, atomic multipole group). The difference of rule matters for the bond_a cluster SAMB.

2. Harmonics of the point groups#

  • Defined once for Oh and D6h (data_top_harmonics, util_top_harmonics) and mapped to the 47 point groups by data_harmonics_mapping (c = ±1 times a top harmonics). MultiPie Ver.2 stores the harmonics of each point group in harmonics.pkl.

  • Result: the keys (X, l, Gamma, n) are identical for all 47 groups (rank <= 11). 32 multiplets have the opposite sign, all from the E2g sign change (section 1).

4. Cluster bases (new)#

cluster_scalar_basis.pkl, cluster_vector_basis.pkl, and cluster_bond_basis.pkl have no counterpart in MultiPie Ver.2. The closest one is harmonics_root_cluster.pkl.

item

MultiPie Ver.2 harmonics_root_cluster

MultiPieGen cluster bases

cluster

one root cluster of 64 points for Oh and D6h

separate Oh (48 points) and D6h (24 points) clusters

representative point

MultiPie Ver.2’s point

site0 = (1, 5, 6), vector0 = unit vector of (6, 1, 5)

content

values of the harmonics of each point group up to rank 11 (not orthonormalized, dependent ones included)

Gram-Schmidt per (irrep, component) of the top group from lower rank, dependent ones dropped: an orthonormal basis (regular representation)

internal type

x = q

x = q and x = g (vector and bond bases)

  • The key format follows MultiPie Ver.2’s site SAMB (scalar), vector SAMB (vector), and bond_a (bond).

  • The bond basis is built by contracting the vector basis with the bond vectors, then Gram-Schmidt in the order rank, k, multiplicity. MultiPie Ver.2 keys each anti-symmetric bond function by the first overlapping vector SAMB; that rule is not used.

  • Result: for every Wyckoff site and bond, each irrep spans the same space as MultiPie Ver.2 (1889 sites, 6663 bonds). The site and bond_s keys are identical.

  • bond_a keys differ in 5990 of 6663 bond Wyckoff positions (keys before the change of type to T/M, i.e. X = Q/G):

    • p only (4866): the top key’s p is kept (e.g. Oh 3a@1o: Q₁(T1u) p = 1 here, p = -1 in MultiPie Ver.2).

    • n (93): another harmonics multiplicity of the same (X, l, Gamma) is used (e.g. C4 4c@1a: Q₂(B) n = 1 here, n = 2 in MultiPie Ver.2).

    • (X, l, Gamma) (1031, 168 groups including 15 point groups): another key for the same space, often of lower rank here (e.g. C4v 4f@1a: G₁(E) here, Q₁(E) in MultiPie Ver.2).

  • The values differ because the representative general point (site0) differs.

8. Other data#

  • Representation matrices: identical for all 241 irreps of the 47 point groups.

  • Products of irreps in the character data of group.pkl (polar-axial conversion, symmetric and anti-symmetric products): computed from the characters (create_group.character_plus) instead of being stored. Two differences from MultiPie Ver.2:

    • Corrected: for E of C4 and S4 and Eg, Eu of C4h (real 2D irreps of complex type), MultiPie Ver.2 has the symmetric product 2A + B and the anti-symmetric product B. The correct values are A + 2B and A: for the basis (x, y), x² + y² is A, x² − y² and xy are B, and x₁y₂ − y₁x₂ is invariant under C4. (E = Ea + Eb with Ea⊗Ea = Eb⊗Eb = B and Ea⊗Eb = A.)

    • The order of the polar-axial conversion follows the character table (10 groups had another order).

  • Active multipoles of MPG (group.pkl): identical after renaming the orbitals (51 of 157 groups have renamed orbitals, e.g. Qf1 → Qfs).

  • Response tensors (response_tensor.pkl): identical to MultiPie Ver.2’s Group.response_tensor_all except for the multipole names (checked when the binary was made, not in the comparison of 2026-11-11).
