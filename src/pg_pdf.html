Point groups#

PDFs of all 47 point groups (32 crystallographic point groups, 5 other settings “-1”, and 10 complex groups “-c”), in multipiegen/pdf/PG/{no}-{tag}/.

  • harmonics: harmonics up to rank 11, with the polar (Q) and axial (G) symbols side by side (the expression is that of the polar harmonics), created by python -m multipiegen.create_pdf.create_pdf_harmonics.

  • character, symmetry operation, Wyckoff site and bond: created by python -m multipiegen.create_pdf.create_pdf_group.
