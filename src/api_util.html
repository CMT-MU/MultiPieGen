Utilities

Utilities#

Loaders of the binary data and reusable logic (multipiegen.util).

multipiegen.util.util_info

Load overall information of groups (multipiegen/binary/info.pkl).

multipiegen.util.util_group

Load group data (multipiegen/binary/group.pkl).

multipiegen.util.util_cluster_basis

Orthonormal (scalar) cluster basis of Oh and D6h clusters (binary: cluster_scalar_basis.pkl).

multipiegen.util.util_cluster_vector_basis

Orthonormal vector cluster basis (internal rank s=1) of Oh and D6h clusters (binary: cluster_vector_basis.pkl).

multipiegen.util.util_bond_cluster_basis

Orthonormal bond cluster basis of Oh and D6h clusters (binary: cluster_bond_basis.pkl).

multipiegen.util.util_harmonics_symbol

Polar and axial symbols of harmonics for all point groups.

multipiegen.util.util_harmonics_multipole

Multipolar harmonics (internal rank s = 1, 2, 3) of point groups with polar (q) and axial (g) internal basis.

multipiegen.util.util_multipolar_harmonics

For multipolar spherical harmonics (sympy).

multipiegen.util.util_symmetry_operation

Symmetry operation matrices (sympy).

multipiegen.util.util_representation_matrix

Representation matrices of point groups from the harmonics (up to rank 11).

multipiegen.util.util_response_tensor

Response tensors of magnetic point groups in terms of active multipoles (up to rank 4).

multipiegen.util.util_binary_data

Binary data for MultiPie (multipiegen/binary_data), in the layout of MultiPie's multipie/binary_data.
